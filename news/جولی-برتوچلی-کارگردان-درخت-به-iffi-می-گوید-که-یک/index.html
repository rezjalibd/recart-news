جولی برتوچلی کارگردان «درخت» به IFFI می‌گوید که یک تهیه‌کننده کانادایی «کفش‌های ایتالیایی» او را دزدیده است.

منبع: Variety • ۳۰ آبان • ۱۵:۲۵ • ۱ دقیقه مطالعه
جولی برتوچلی، فیلمساز فرانسوی، که «درخت» جشنواره فیلم کن را در سال 2010 تعطیل کرد، گفت که اقتباس برنامه ریزی شده او از رمان کفش های ایتالیایی هنینگ مانکل پس از دزدیدن فیلم توسط یک تهیه کننده کانادایی از بین رفت و او قصد دارد فیلم بعدی خود را در هند فیلمبرداری کند. برتوچ...

French filmmaker Julie Bertuccelli , whose “The Tree” closed the Cannes Film Festival in 2010, said her planned adaptation of Henning Mankell’s novel “Italian Shoes” fell apart after a Canadian producer “stole the film” from her, and that she plans to shoot her next feature in India.

Bertuccelli was speaking at “From Witness to Change: The Social Responsibility of Documentary Cinema,” a masterclass at the International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Goa, moderated by Indian filmmaker Haobam Paban Kumar. Related Stories IDFA Head on How ‘You Can See Everything’ Phenomenon Can Pump New Blood Into Theatrical Doc Releases: ‘What Else Would This Audience See?’ Berlin Winner Tatiana Huezo Close to Completing Road Movie ‘Galerna’ Following Awards Boost: ‘I’m Dying to Finish It’

Responding to an audience question, Bertuccelli said she set out to adapt the Mankell book five years ago. She wrote the script and cast the film, which was due to shoot in Canada, before the pandemic brought everything to a halt.

“In fact there is a bad Canadian producer who stole the film from me,” Bertuccelli said. She did not name the producer and declined to give the full account. She said she was forced to cancel the project, adding: “I worked for quite four years, five years for nothing because they stole from me, they stole, you know, the movie and I could not do it. So it was very hard.”

Bertuccelli turned to the directors in the room. “It’s a fight against you, against yourself, because you need to work, you need even when you are depressed or you have doubts, you need to continue, because the only thing that makes you a director is that you go on and you never, you know, you never stop your desire,” she said.

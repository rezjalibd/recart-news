در اینجا همه چیزهایی است که GTA+ در GTA 6 به شما می دهد: آیا ارزش آن 8 دلار در ماه است؟

منبع: Engadget • ۲۹ آبان • ۲۱:۵۷ • ۳ دقیقه مطالعه
عضویت +GTA چند ویژگی اضافی به GTA 6 با 8 دلار در ماه اضافه می کند. در اینجا آمده است که چه چیزی شامل می شود و کدام بازیکنان به احتمال زیاد به ارزش پول خود می رسند. GTA+ چند امتیاز به GTA 6 اضافه می کند و یکی از آنها در حال حاضر بازیکنان را تحریک می کند. بازی‌های Rocksta...

GTA+ adds a few perks to GTA 6 and one of them is already stirring up players.

Rockstar Games Add Engadget on Google: Preferred Source Google Discover A lot has changed in gaming in the 13 years since Grand Theft Auto 5 debuted. That includes the explosion of live-service games and gaming subscriptions, and the Grand Theft Auto franchise has gotten in on both with GTA Online and the GTA+ membership. Now that Grand Theft Auto 6 is out , GTA+ members get some additional benefits in the new game on top of what the subscription already offers in GTA Online .

If you're new to GTA+, you can sign up for it in-game or through your console's digital storefront. Digital pre-orders of GTA 6 on PlayStation and Xbox also included a free month of GTA+ to try it out. If you didn't pre-order or you want to keep the subscription after the free month ends, it costs $8 a month.

Rockstar Games That $8 gets you a few perks in GTA 6 , but nothing game-changing. You'll get 10 percent more money for any eligible vehicles you sell in the game and a 10 percent discount at a variety of stores. You can also use in-game transport options like scooters, bikes and public transit for free and get a premium membership to the RydeMe rideshare service. Together, those perks add up to a few cheaper ways to get around GTA 6's huge map and an easier time making cash. That can make a big difference in your progress early in the game, since cheats don't unlock until you beat the story .

The biggest and most controversial benefit is one you can claim only after reaching GTA 6 's third chapter. At that point, you'll get the '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy, a free sports car, plus a garage in the Crosstown area. Locking exclusive items in a single-player game behind a monthly subscription is never popular, and players have already voiced their displeasure on Reddit.

Rockstar Games For most players, the GTA+ subscription fee probably isn't worth it for what it adds to GTA 6 alone. From the start, GTA+ has focused mostly on GTA Online , giving members in-game currency and discounts, new vehicles and garages to store them in, and a rotating suite of items and features. For dedicated GTA Online players who also picked up GTA 6 , there's a much better case for the subscription.

The game library included with GTA+ might make it worth it for more people. Anyone with an active subscription also gets access to a selection of Rockstar's other games, including Red Dead Redemption and GTA 5 . Of course, you might not have time for any of those until you finish a campaign that can run 80 hours , but the library does make the $8 subscription much easier to justify.

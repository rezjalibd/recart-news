Personally, I've been keeping an eye on all the best deals on home gym equipment that you can snag from now until the end of Cyber Monday (Nov. 30).

Whether you're shopping for cardio or strength training, whether your home gym set-up is big or small, there are major discounts on brands like Peloton , PowerBlocks , Whoop , and more. Below are my favorite fitness deals I've seen so far, and I'll keep updating this list until Cyber Monday ends.

For more health gadgets, make sure to check out the best Black Friday 2026 deals on fitness trackers and smartwatches .

If you're looking for at-home cardio, you can't go wrong with joining Peloton's extensive ecosystem. Bikes are no doubt Peloton's signature offering, and the Cross-Training series ( new as of 2025 ) includes swiveling screens and upgraded hardware compared to the original Bikes. Looking at Peloton's site, you can snag a refurbished (or rental) Bike+ for as low as $995 . (The more basic Bike is unavailable, according to the Peloton website.)

Peloton Cross-Training Bike , $1,295 (down from $1,695, so $400 off)

And if you're looking for a rower, Peloton's Cross-Training Row+ is $2,845 (down from $3,495, so $650 off)

Sticking with Peloton for a few more deals, the brand's treadmills (which Peloton calls Treads) are also available in a newer Cross-Training version. Here are the deals I'm seeing:

Peloton Tread Flex , which folds up to save space: $1,995 (was $2,195, so $200 off)

Peloton Tread Vision , with a camera for form analysis: $2,795 (was $3,495, so $700 off)

Peloton Tread+ Vision , with a cushioned slat belt and sled push mode: $5,395 (was $6,695, so $1,300 off)

For more treadmill deals, NordicTrack always has reliable discounts on a wide range of options. The NordicTrack T6.5s is a solid budget option for a foldable treadmill, currently on sale for $616, down from $799. You could also spring for the tilting 10” touchscreen on the NordicTrack T10 for $949.05, down from $1,299.

This YOSUDA PRO magnetic exercise bike is $251.74, normally $329.99. With magnetic resistance, you can count on the bike to be quiet enough for a home gym setup.

This MERACH UltraWalk W60 walking pad is on sale for $282.12, down from $349.99. Designed to fit under your desk and be easy to store, this walking pad still gets up to a decent 12% incline at speeds up to four miles per hour.

Right now, my favorite set—and a Lifehacker "Gift of the Year" award winner —the PowerBlock Elite EXP Adjustable Dumbbells , is $339.99, down from its usual $399.99 price tag. Bowflex Results Series 552 dumbbells are $328.99 for a pair, normally $429.99. These can adjust from 5 to 52 pounds.

These UNNMIIY adjustable dumbbells are $151.98 for the 90-pound set, normally $259.99. These can adjust from 2 to 45 pounds per dumbbell, and they have a 5-in-1 design so you can use them as dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, and even push-up stands.

These FEIERDUN adjustable dumbbells are $227.98 for the 110-pound set, normally $349.99. These have my favorite type of adjustment mechanism: a one-hand rotating dial.

This BowFlex SelectTech 840 Adjustable Kettlebell is $127.49, normally $149.99. With the turn of a dial, you can easily adjust from 8 to 40 pounds.

This cast-iron adjustable kettlebell is $45.99, normally $59.99. This kettlebell starts with a 15-pound weighted handle with four non-weighted removable spacer disks, a five-pound bottom plate, and a center pin to support additional weight plates up to 50 pounds.

For traditional weights, these CAP Rubber Coated Dumbbell Weights are $88.34 for a 50-pound pair, normally $115.99. CAP is a fan-favorite brand for strength training at home.

For your recovery needs, I'll spotlight that Therabody Prime 6th Gen Deep Tissue Massage Gun is $194.99, normally $329.99. As a marathon runner, it's no hyperbole to say that massage guns utterly transformed my post-workout routine, and TheraBody is a brand name I swear by.

Black Friday sales officially begin Friday, November 27, 2026, and run throughout “Cyber Week,” the five-day period that runs from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday , November 30, 2026. But Black Friday and Cyber Monday dates have expanded as retailers compete for customers. You can get the same Black Friday sales early , and we expect sales to wind down by December 2, 2026.

Black Friday used to be bigger for major retailers and more expensive tech and appliances, while Cyber Monday was better for cheaper tech and gave smaller businesses a chance to compete online. Nowadays, though, the distinction is almost meaningless. Every major retailer will offer sales on both days, and the smart move is to know what you want, do your research, or refer to guides like our Black Friday and Cyber Monday coverage that have done all the vetting for you.

Yes, Amazon has Black Friday sales, but when it comes to Peloton, not all the equipment is available on Amazon. Check the Peloton website or other retailers for all your options. (I've linked each one above to a place where you can buy it—some on Amazon and some from Peloton directly.) Refer to guides like our live blog that vet deals for you. And if you have an Amazon Prime membership, make the most of it .

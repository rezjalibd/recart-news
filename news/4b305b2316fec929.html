Tech companies are racing to figure out how AI can make everyday work a little easier.

From notetakers to webcams that follow your movements, here are six AI-powered gadgets worth checking out if you want to upgrade your workspace.

If you’re constantly scribbling notes during meetings, the Flowtica Scribe could save you from having to type everything up afterward.

The AI-powered pen combines traditional handwriting with audio recording. You can use its built-in gel pen to take notes, tap the side to highlight important points, and press the end of the pen to start recording audio. Once the meeting is over, its AI can transcribe the conversation, summarize key takeaways, and create a to-do list based on what was discussed.

You can even ask the AI questions about the meeting to find specific information without having to go through the entire recording.

The Scribe comes with a built-in microphone and 32GB of onboard storage. It can also identify up to 15 different speakers and supports 92 languages.

Anker’s AI-powered recording pin, Soundcore Work , is designed to capture conversations, meetings, and quick voice memos without taking up much space on your desk.

Priced at $159 (currently reduced to $99 in the Amazon Black Friday sale), the coin-sized device comes with a puck-shaped charging case and can record for up to eight hours on a single charge, extending to 32 hours when used with its case. Anker also claims a recording range of up to 5 meters, making it useful for capturing conversations around a meeting table.

The companion app handles transcription and uses AI to turn recordings into more organized notes and summaries. Users also get 300 minutes of free audio transcription each month.

If you need more recording capacity or access to additional AI features, Anker offers optional subscription plans, including Pro ($99 per year) and Unlimited ($239 per year).

Logitech’s MX Keypad brings AI shortcuts directly to your desk, with dedicated buttons that let you launch your preferred AI assistant or trigger specific productivity tools with a single press. If you frequently switch between different apps and AI assistants, having these shortcuts within reach could make your workday easier.

The keypad can be configured to work with tools such as GitHub Copilot and Claude Code, along with a range of popular productivity and creative apps, such as OpenAI Codex, Microsoft Teams, PowerPoint, Excel, Notion, Slack, Photoshop, Final Cut Pro, Zoom, and Figma.

Amazon’s Kindle Scribe Colorsoft brings color to the company’s digital notetaking lineup and offers some neat AI features designed to clean up your messy notes.

Starting at $479 , the device is aimed at people who regularly work with digital documents, including students, researchers, and professionals who prefer annotating files rather than printing them out. It lets you highlight important passages, mark up documents, organize handwritten notes, and sketch out ideas.

Notably, the Scribe Colorsoft incorporates AI-powered tools to clean up messy handwriting, straighten highlighting and underlining, summarize notes, and help turn scattered thoughts into more organized information. Its search feature can look across your saved notes to find specific details and help identify connections between different entries.

If you’re tired of adjusting your webcam during video calls, Insta360’s Link 2 Pro aims to take care of that for you.

Priced at $249 (discounted to $199 for Black Friday ), this AI-powered webcam combines 4K video capture with intelligent tracking and automatic framing. Its smart video tracker can follow your movements and adjust the camera’s position to keep you in view, even when you move around your workspace, which is particularly useful for people who regularly give presentations, teach online, or switch between sitting at their desk and standing during video calls.

The camera also offers group tracking, which can come in handy during team meetings or presentations involving multiple people. AI-powered noise cancellation is another useful addition, helping reduce distracting background sounds during calls.

Your desk could probably use a little intelligence, too. Autonomous Desk Pro is an adjustable standing desk designed to encourage healthier habits throughout the workday.

Beyond switching between sitting and standing positions, the AI-powered desk uses built-in sensors to monitor your activity and remind you when it’s time to move, stretch, or change positions. It can track how much time you spend sitting versus standing, giving you a better idea of your daily work habits.

The AI features go a step further by allowing you to ask questions about your activity. For example, you can check how long you’ve been sitting, compare your activity with the previous day, or get information about the room’s CO2 levels to help determine whether it’s time to open a window.

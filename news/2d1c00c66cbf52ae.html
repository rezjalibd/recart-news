این ترفند سفر سریع GTA 6 قیمتی دارد

منبع: Engadget • ۲۹ آبان • ۲۳:۰۲ • ۲ دقیقه مطالعه
هیچ کمبودی در گزینه های سفر در GTA 6 وجود ندارد، اما کدام یک شما را سریعتر به آنجا می رساند؟ عدنان احمد برای Engadget افزودن Engadget در Google: منبع ترجیحی Google Discover با Grand Theft Auto 6، بازیکنان می توانند بار دیگر Vice City را کاوش کنند—تنها این بار، این شهر ب...

Calling a taxi isn't your only option.

Adnan Ahmed for Engadget Add Engadget on Google: Preferred Source Google Discover With Grand Theft Auto 6 , players can once again explore Vice City—only this time, the city is part of the much larger fictional state of Leonida. It's no surprise that the GTA 6 map dwarfs the map of GTA Vice City , but it's also estimated to be roughly twice the size of GTA 5's already impressive open world. This means you'll be driving around quite a lot.

The first few hours of the game are spent in the Key Lento region, but even this relatively small part of the map feels enormous. New regions in different counties are unlocked as the game progresses. While driving for 30 minutes from one end of the map to the other is always a fun option, GTA 6 lets you fast travel if you want to shave time off your journeys and reach your objectives faster. Unlike some games that let you open the map and instantly teleport to a discovered location, Rockstar makes you work a little for it.

The easiest way to get started with fast travel is to order a taxi. Press the up button on your D-pad to bring up the phone. Navigate to Contacts > All Contacts and call Paradise Taxi . A cab should arrive at your location in a few seconds. Get in the taxi and select a destination on the map. Press L2 or LT to focus on the driver. You can either pay $5 to get the driver to drive faster or spend $100 to skip the journey entirely. This is in addition to the fare for the ride, which varies depending on how far your destination is.

Among the many things you can do in GTA 6 , you can also use public transport. You can locate bus stops and train stations by navigating to the Vehicles tab on the map. Get on a train or a bus, take a seat and choose to skip the journey. This will open the map and ask you to select a destination. Public transport is considerably cheaper than hailing a cab, but you're limited to fast travel between designated bus stops and train stations.

RydeMe and Paradise Taxi remain the quickest ways to get around Leonida, but they cost a lot, especially early in the game with limited funds. For instance, both taxi services charge over $750 if you're hailing a ride from Mariana County to Lummox County—the two regions at opposite ends of the map. Unsurprisingly, train and bus rides are far more economical, costing a fraction of what you'd pay for a taxi.

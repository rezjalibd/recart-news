Editorial Director/Box Office Editor

Lili Reinhart is in talks to star in and executive produce Amazon MGM Studios and Mattel Studios’ Polly Pocket , we understand.

The news follows Emily in Paris star Lily Collins departure from the project a while ago due to a scheduling conflict. She remains an executive producer on the project.

Clea DuVall boarded Polly Pocket as director over the summer. The pic is being shot for Prime Video.

Producers also include Mattel Studios’ Robbie Brenner, and Hello Sunshine’s Reese Witherspoon and Lauren Neustadter. Mattel Studios, Hello Sunshine’s Emily Ferenbach, Jeff Waxman, and Jennifer Madeloff are EPs. Sweethearts scribes Jordan Weiss and Dan Brier wrote the script, inspired by Mattel’s popular micro-doll line. Watch on Deadline

Polly Pocket was first introduced in 1989. In 2018, Mattel reintroduced the micro-doll line with a product offering that pays homage to the brand’s origins, with updates for a new generation.

Riverdale star Reinhart most recently headlined The Love Hypothesis for Amazon MGM Studios. The romantic comedy, which is Certified Fresh on Rotten Tomatoes, debuted at #2 on Nielsen’s movies chart with over 400M minutes streamed on Prime Video and held the #1 spot on Prime Video’s weekly rankings for three weeks. Her edgy horror comedy Forbidden Fruits world premiered at SXSW back in March.

Amazon didn’t respond to request for comment on today’s news.

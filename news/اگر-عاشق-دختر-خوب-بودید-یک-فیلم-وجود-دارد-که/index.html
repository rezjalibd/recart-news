اگر عاشق «دختر خوب» بودید، یک فیلم وجود دارد که باید در ادامه ببینید

منبع: Lifehacker • ۴ آذر • ۲۱:۳۰ • ۲ دقیقه مطالعه
ممکن است از پیوندهای موجود در این صفحه کمیسیون دریافت کنیم. دلایل زیادی وجود دارد که فیلم The Good Daughter Peacocks بسیار قابل تماشا است. اجراها، برای یکی رز بیرن و مگان فاهی، در نقش خواهران جدا شده سام و شارلوت کوین فوق العاده است. اما این داستان همچنین یک داستان عالی...

There are plenty of reasons Peacock’s The Good Daughter is so damn watchable. The performances, for one—Rose Byrne and Meghann Fahy are terrific as estranged sisters Sam and Charlotte Quinn. But it’s also a great story, tracing the corrosive effect of a years-ago trauma on the women and their lawyer father (Brendan Gleeson) amid the slow revelation of many extremely dark secrets. If you want more of that flavor of trauma-driven drama with a tight focus on family bonds, there’s one movie you should watch: 2015's Dark Places.

You want buried secrets? Dark Places has seriously buried secrets. Adapted from the Gillian Flynn novel, the film follows Libby Day (Charlize Theron), who survived the mass murder of her family in 1985. The crime is blamed on her brother Ben, a moody loner whose conviction was based in large part on Libby’s own testimony. For a long time, Libby is financially sustained by donations from people who sympathize with her plight, but three decades after the murders, the money has dried up, and Libby is desperate—so desperate, she agrees to help out a “true crime” club that believes her brother is innocent and is working toward his release.

Like The Good Daughter, Dark Places is anchored by a sibling relationship reshaped by the impact of horrifying violence. Libby and Ben’s experience mirrors Sam and Charlie’s in many ways, including in the way both Libby and Ben carry secrets for years after the terrible events that took family from them. The stories are also both compelling mysteries, slowly revealing the shocking truths behind one horrific night, and ultimately allowing the characters to better understand one another.

Dark Places also explores the insular, suffocating nature of small-town life, and how rumor and gossip become a poisonous presence in people’s lives. Libby, Ben, Sam, and Charlie all have to deal with others' assumptions and revelations that they've been betrayed by those closest to them, leading to powerful resolutions. If you loved The Good Daughter, you’ll love this movie. Stream Dark Places on The Roku Channel or Kanopy, or rent it on Prime Video.

Gadgets Verge Shopping Black Friday Here are the Apple deals actually worth picking up ahead of Black Friday From AirPods to Apple Watches, many Apple devices are already seeing notable discounts ahead of November 27th.

Sheena Vasani Nov 20, 2026, 5:33 PM UTC Link Share If you buy something from a link, The Verge may earn a commission. See our ethics statement.

Sheena Vasani writes about tech news, reviews gadgets, and helps readers save money by highlighting deals and product recommendations for The Verge . A new pair of AirPods or the latest Apple Watch can make a great holiday gift, but they can also take a pretty big dent out of your budget. That’s especially true this year, as rising hardware prices have made MacBooks and other Apple devices even more expensive. Fortunately, Black Friday offers a chance to save, especially since great deals are already available on a wide range of Apple devices. Some discounts even extend to recent releases like the Apple Watch Series 12 and touchscreen MacBook Pro , which have dropped to new lows.

Most of the discounts below are promoted as Black Friday deals, but we’ve also included a few that aren’t technically part of the shopping event but have “coincidentally” dropped in price in the last week or so. Black Friday isn’t until November 27th, however, so some prices could fall further in the coming days. Apple is also kicking off its own Black Friday shopping event that day, offering up to $300 in gift cards with select purchases. That said, if you don’t want to wait, we think the deals below are already worth taking advantage of.

Beats’ fitness-focused earbuds feature ear hooks designed to stay put during even the most intense workouts as well as built-in heart rate monitoring. They also deliver relatively bass-rich sound for their design, along with solid noise cancellation, wireless charging, and AirPods features like adaptive audio thanks to Apple’s H2 chip. The latest Beats Studio Pro feature improved noise cancellation, sound quality, and comfort. They also support lossless audio over USB-C and offer native support for a range of iOS and Android features like other Beats headphones. Read our review .

The Beats 360 are ANC headphones for people who prefer headphones over earbuds at the gym. They’re Beats’ first IP-rated cans, with ear cushions that you can easily remove for cleaning (or style points). They also stay in place during most exercises, while still delivering solid bass. Read our review.

Apple’s most affordable laptop uses the same A18 Pro chip found in the iPhone 16 Pro. It features a vibrant 13-inch display, surprisingly good speakers, and 8GB of RAM, making it a solid option for students and anyone who needs a laptop for everyday tasks. Read our review .

Apple’s latest MacBook Air features an M5 chip and starts with 512GB of storage, with significantly faster performance than prior generations. It’s still an excellent machine despite the higher base price, especially given the battery life and speakers. Read our review .

The Apple Watch Series 12 introduces AI-powered Audio Intelligence features, including sound recognition alerts, Live Rewind, and Siri Recap. It can also track your heart rate every five seconds, even when you’re not exercising, and offers faster charging than prior models. Read our review .

Apple’s latest AirTags feature an upgraded ultra-wideband chip for more precise location tracking, a noticeably louder speaker, and an expanded Bluetooth range. Read our review.

The Baseus PicoGo AM52 is a 10,000mAh magnetic power bank that supports fast 25W Qi2.2 wireless charging and up to 45W wired charging via its built-in USB-C cable. It was one of the fastest wireless chargers we tested, bringing an iPhone 17 Pro Max to 65 percent in an hour. Read our guide to the best Qi2 batteries.

The Icemag 3 can wirelessly deliver up to 25W of power to compatible smartphones and up to 35W wired charging via USB-C. The 10,000mAh Qi2 charger can also act as a foldable kickstand, comes with an integrated USB-C cable that serves as a lanyard, and sports a transparent design with colorful RGB lighting.

Designed to pair with a Mac, the latest Apple Studio Display features a sharp 5K Retina display, a great 12-megapixel Center Stage camera that keeps you in frame during video calls, and a good six-speaker sound system with spatial audio. Sadly, it only offers two Thunderbolt 5 ports in addition to a pair of USB-C ports. Read our review.

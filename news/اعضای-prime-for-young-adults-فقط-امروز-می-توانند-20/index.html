When you buy through our links, Business Insider may earn an affiliate commission.Learn more Amazon; Gabrielle Chase/Business Insider Amazon's fresh membership tier, Prime for Young Adults , is already a value-packed offering for college students and newcomers to the job market, cutting Prime's cost in half to make the service more affordable.

It's open to users between the ages of 18 and 24 years-old, and with this limited-time Amazon Black Friday deal, members can score 20% cash back on popular items.You don't need a Prime Visa card to get these benefits; by buying the items included in the Black Friday promotion, Amazon will automatically grant Prime for Young Adult members 20% (four times the normal 5% cash back) of their spend to use toward future purchases.

It's an incredible opportunity not only to save on popular items in tech and personal care, but also to take a chunk out of future purchases when shopping with the retailer.How to get 20% cash back with Prime for Young Adults To score your sweet perks, you'll first want to make sure you're enrolled as a Prime for Young Adults member.

You won't even have to pay for your membership just yet — Amazon is offering newcomers their first six months free.Once you're enrolled, you'll need to shop the included items in the promotion to get your 20%.

Year-round, Young Adult members already get 5% cash back, which you can track in your account details.The same rings true for items not included in the Black Friday deal.

Shop these items for 20% cash back Browse more cashback-eligible items on Amazon.Who qualifies for Prime for Young Adults? The replacement for Prime Student, Prime for Young Adults, targets a similar demographic and does not require verified institutional enrollment status.

Anyone between the ages of 18 and 24 years old can sign up for this membership.Right now, newcomers can also get a free six-month trial.

How much is Prime for Young Adults? After the initial free trial, Prime for Young Adults costs $7.That's half the price of normal Prime membership.
